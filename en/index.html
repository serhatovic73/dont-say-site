Simple rules

How to play

1

Split into teams

Make two or more teams. On each turn, one player of the active team describes.

2

Describe without the taboos

The words under the card are off limits. The more words your team guesses before time runs out, the more points!

3

Let one slip: TABOO!

If a taboo word is said, the other team hits the buzzer and you lose a point. Then it is their turn.

Your way

One phone or online

One phone

Pass the phone around. No internet needed, play with 4 people or a whole crowd.

Online room

Invite friends with a room code, everyone plays on their own phone. Even from another country.

Voice mode

Play together in person or on a voice call.

Text mode

Type your clues; the app catches taboo words automatically.
